Executive summary

  • Dates. Preferred trip 15–18 February 2027 (3 nights); the requested 7-night hotel pricing case is 15–22 February 2027 (Monday to Monday). Both are carried forward; the duration choice is an open decision.
  • MICHELIN. The live list checked 25 November 2026 shows 5 three-star, 14 two-star and 50 one-star NYC restaurants. The catalogue contains all 69 starred rows plus 3 Guide-listed additions with no star. The 14 December ceremony may change distinctions before the trip.
  • Hotels. Ten named Queen/King room types with private bathrooms are verified on primary property pages. Eight have dated Booking.com pre-tax snapshots for 15–22 Feb; two remain unpriced. These are not official hotel quotes, and complete checkout totals are not captured.
  • Activities. Fifteen attractions and events include official hours and exceptions where published, including Wollman Rink's 15 Feb Community Day, Bryant Park's 28 Feb rink cutoff and the announced 21 Feb Chinatown parade.
  • Travel. A stored sample of 25 Google Maps transit-mode routes (five hotel origins × five MICHELIN clusters) returned 3–37 minutes on 25 Nov. These are “leave now” estimates, not future schedules; some short results were walk-only.
  • Prices & social proof. Nineteen restaurants have current official menu/booking-channel prices for two, captured 25–26 Nov; recheck for the trip. Third-party review links are labelled, and official account links are included only where a venue source confirms them. MICHELIN's $ band is never converted to a dollar total.

How to read the tables

Official source links to the Michelin page, government page or venue's own page used for that row. Official site is the venue's own website where one was published by the source. blank — unverified means no official confirmation was found. A flag explains why. Review videos are third-party and are not treated as official endorsements.

On a phone, each table row becomes a card. Use the search box to filter. Select a hotel as route origin on the Hotels page; all "Route" links then start from that hotel.
