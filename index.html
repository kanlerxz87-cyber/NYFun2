Executive summary

  • Michelin. The canonical restaurant table contains 72 records: 69 starred venues in the 25 Nov 2026 live-list snapshot (5 three-star, 14 two-star, 50 one-star) plus 3 Guide-listed additions without stars. Fifteen have an official-source couple menu total recorded; prices are food-only unless the row says otherwise and may change before the trip.
  • Hotels. Twelve candidates are listed. Four have dated 15–22 Feb 2027 totals from a third-party booking engine; eight are blank. These dated snapshots are not direct hotel quotes. The canonical dataset has not established the cheapest week across the full travel window.
  • Transit. The canonical site's hotel ranking does not claim a verified count of Michelin restaurants within one hour. A separate, detailed planning kit reports measured transit legs and a multi-week hotel comparison; it is preserved separately, with its own data and flags, and should be treated as an independent research set rather than merged canonical data.

How to read the tables

Official source links to the primary page used for a row. Official site links to the venue's own website where available. blank — unverified means the value was not confirmed; consult the linked flag. Review videos are third-party and are not endorsements.

On a phone, each table row becomes a card. Use the search box to filter. Select a hotel on the Hotels page to set it as the origin for the live route-query links.
