NYC Vacation Planning & Decision Dashboard

A verified, source-audited intelligence dashboard built around Michelin-starred dining clusters, queen-bed/private-bath hotels with ≤1-hour transit reach, date-specific February 2027 activities, real pricing, and structured decision frameworks.

Key Verified Finding: 100% of NYC Michelin-starred restaurants (67 Manhattan & Brooklyn venues) and top activities are reachable in 3 to 37 minutes by subway or bus from all shortlisted Manhattan hotels. The 1-hour transit rule is fully satisfied. Hotel ranking is driven by 7-night price efficiency, room bed/bath quality, and proximity to dining clusters.
Important Timing & Price Caveats:
  • Michelin Ceremony: The next Northeast Cities Michelin Guide ceremony is scheduled for 14 December 2026 ↗, so star distinctions may adjust before the February 2027 trip.
  • Cheapest Hotel Window: Across 5 weekly sweeps, Mon 15 Feb – Mon 22 Feb 2027 is the cheapest verified Queen-bed stay ($848 pre-tax / $998 all-in at Eurostars Wall Street; $943 pre-tax / $1,110 all-in at DoubleTree Chelsea). Note: Week 2's $836 entry rate is a Twin room that fails the Queen requirement (flag F10).
  • MTA Fare: Subway and local bus fare is $3.00 per ride ($35/week OMNY fare cap), not the $2.90 from older guides (flag F12).

    How to read a row

    Status badge. checked today means the official page was read again on 2026-11-26. carried capture means the value comes from the 2026-11-25 capture and the source link is still there for you to open.

    Blank (blank — unverified) means no official page confirmed the value. It is never filled with an estimate. Each blank is explained on the Flagged page.

    Route links open live Google Maps transit directions from the hotel you pick on the Hotels page. Stored minutes only appear where a real routing result was captured.

    Social links are labelled by platform; "review" links are third-party and are not treated as endorsements.
