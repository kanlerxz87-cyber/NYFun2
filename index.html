Executive summary

  • Dates. Preferred trip 15–18 February 2027 (3 nights); the requested 7-night hotel pricing case is 15–22 February 2027 (Monday to Monday). Both are carried forward; the duration choice is an open decision.
  • Michelin. The live Michelin list checked 25 November 2026 shows 5 three-star, 14 two-star and 50 one-star NYC restaurants. This site details all 72 rows (69 starred + 3 Guide-listed) with 19 verified couple food totals from official menu and booking pages. Guide-only and starred rows are clearly separated.
  • Hotels. Sixteen candidates are listed; ten have an official room/bath specification and eight carry a dated 7-night total for 15–22 Feb 2027. Four all-in totals were captured independently by two sessions and agree. The cheapest qualifying week captured so far is 15–22 Feb 2027 at $848 pre-tax / ~$998 all-in; the $836 following week has twin beds and fails the brief.
  • Travel times. Twenty-five measured door-to-door legs put every tested Manhattan hotel within 3–37 minutes of all five Michelin clusters, so the one-hour rule does not separate them. Price and room quality become the tie-break. See Research & integration.
  • Activities. Eighteen attractions, parks and date-specific events are listed with opening days, hours and exceptions where published. Highlights include the one-day Chinatown parade (21 Feb 2027), Bryant Park's 28 Feb cutoff and Wollman Rink's 16 Mar cutoff.
  • Social proof. 86 activity and 102 restaurant review/social links are carried across the tables. Links confirmed by two independent session audits are labelled cross-confirmed; others name the capturing session. Review links are never used to establish hours or prices.
  • Costs. Couple totals appear only where an official adult price was published and read; Michelin's $ band is not a dollar amount and is never converted into one. Prices captured in Nov 2026 must be re-checked for a Feb–Mar 2027 trip.

How to read the tables

Official source links to the Michelin page, government page or venue's own page used for that row. Official site is the venue's own website where one was published by the source. Days & hours are separated, and Price source opens the exact menu or booking page behind a couple total. blank — unverified means no official confirmation was found. A flag explains why. Review videos are third-party and are not treated as official endorsements.

On a phone, each table row becomes a card. Use the search box to filter. Select a hotel as route origin on the Hotels page; all "Route" links then start from that hotel.
