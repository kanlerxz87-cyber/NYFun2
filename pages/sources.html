Method & provenance

Sources and verification rules

Verification rules used

  • Every table row links to an official or primary source: MICHELIN Guide, a government site (NPS, NYC DOT, NYC Parks) or the venue's own website.
  • Hotel rate links are explicitly different: Booking.com snapshots are third-party aggregator observations, not hotel quotes. Their capture date, room/rate plan and exclusions are recorded; full checkout totals are not inferred.
  • A value is recorded only when that source states it. Arithmetic on a published per-person menu/admission price is labelled as such.
  • If a value cannot be confirmed, the cell is blank and an explanation is added to the Flags page.
  • Review videos are labelled third-party; they are not used to establish hours, prices or ownership.
  • Neighbourhood labels are derived from the published street address and marked "address-based grouping".
  • Any stated travel time is linked to a stored Google Maps result and labelled with its capture time; a live “leave now” estimate is not a future schedule or guarantee.
