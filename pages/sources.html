Method & provenance

Sources and verification rules

Verification rules used

  • Every table row links to an official or primary source: Michelin Guide, a government site (NPS, NYC DOT, NYC Parks) or the venue's own website.
  • A value is recorded only when that source states it. Arithmetic on a published adult price is labelled as such.
  • If a value cannot be confirmed, the cell is blank and an explanation is added to the Flags page.
  • Review videos are labelled third-party; they are not used to establish hours, prices or ownership.
  • Neighbourhood labels are derived from the published street address and marked "address-based grouping".
  • No travel duration is claimed without a stored routing result.
