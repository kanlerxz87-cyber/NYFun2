A 7-night skeleton that respects opening days

Day by day from verified hours and February dates, with the 3-night variant and the checks to run before booking.

This is a skeleton, not a booking. Each day uses only venues whose opening day is verified for that weekday. Restaurant suggestions are drawn from rows whose published hours include that day; where hours are blank the row is marked "check".

7-night structure (15–22 Feb 2027)

Mon 15 Feb — arrive, Presidents' Day. Museums run holiday hours; expect crowds. Keep the evening free or book a low-commitment dinner. Do not book anything non-refundable until the hotel is confirmed.

Tue 16 Feb — The Met. Open 10:00–17:00 (closed Wednesdays). Pair with Central Park or the Guggenheim on Fifth Avenue. Evening: a Flatiron/Midtown dinner — Eleven Madison Park ($770 for two) or Gramercy Tavern's tavern menu ($156).

Wed 17 Feb — MoMA. The Met is closed today; MoMA is open 10:30–17:30. Afternoon: Top of the Rock or SUMMIT. Evening: Cote's Butcher's Feast ($168) or The Modern ($230 lunch prix fixe, $370 dinner).

Thu 18 Feb — downtown. Statue of Liberty ferry (last statue entry 15:30), 9/11 Memorial, Staten Island Ferry at sunset (free), or the Brooklyn Bridge walk. Evening: Jungsik ($670) or Le Coucou ($440).

Fri 19 Feb — late museum day. MoMA is open to 20:30; the Met to 21:00. Use the morning for the High Line and Chelsea Market, the evening for a starred dinner — Aquavit ($390) or Gabriel Kreuther ($400).

Sat 20 Feb — flexible. AMNH (dinosaurs + Gilder Center), Wollman Rink (off-peak $16 if the date is off-peak; $28 peak / $40 prime), or a second museum. Evening: Masa ($990 lunch / $1,500 table) or Le Bernardin ($436 dinner prix fixe).

Sun 21 Feb — Lunar New Year parade, 13:00. Chinatown. This is the single fixed event of the trip and the strongest reason to keep the 7-night window. Book a light dinner, not a tasting menu, given street-food temptation: Semma or Crown Shy are the cheaper starred options.

Mon 22 Feb — depart. Morning at Bryant Park's rink (open to 28 Feb) or a final walk before the flight.

3-night variant (15–18 Feb)

If you keep the preferred Mon 15 – Thu 18 window, keep Tue (Met), Wed (MoMA) and Thu (downtown) and drop the Friday–Sunday blocks. You lose the 21 Feb parade and the Bryant Park weekend, which is the main trade-off to weigh. No 3-night hotel total exists in the verified sources yet — that is question Q5/S5.

Before you book anything

  1. Confirm 3 or 7 nights, and confirm the hotel (Q1, Q2).
  2. Re-check MICHELIN stars after 14 Dec 2026 (Q4).
  3. Check each chosen restaurant's own page for its February hours and holiday closures — several close Sunday/Monday and the data marks those that are unknown.
  4. Route each restaurant from the chosen hotel at the intended time. The stored minutes are morning/evening snapshots from 25 Nov 2026, not timetable guarantees.
  5. Book the 21 Feb parade day loosely: streets close and restaurants fill.
