Planning logic

Itinerary & the one-hour travel goal

A route sample now tests the ≤1-hour goal, with its limits made explicit. The saved figures are Google Maps “leave now” estimates from the capture moment—not February 2027 transit schedules or guarantees.

What the route sample shows

  • 25 legs: five hotel origins × five representative MICHELIN clusters, captured on 25 Nov 2026 between 17:14 and 17:31 ET. Returned estimates range from 3 to 37 minutes; all were under one hour.
  • Hotels tested: Eurostars Wall Street, DoubleTree Chelsea, The Frederick Tribeca, The Evelyn NoMad and The Benjamin Royal Sonesta. The first four are on the current hotel shortlist; The Benjamin is a separate route-test reference.
  • Route mode: Google Maps was queried in transit mode, but several very short trips returned walk-only results. Do not treat these as pure subway/bus legs where a ride is required.
  • Open the saved T11 matrix ↗ for each time, route line and directions URL. It is a snapshot, not a timetable.
  • The MTA's official subway and bus maps ↗ identify the lines serving each area; live route links are embedded in every restaurant and activity row.

What is not verified

This sample does not cover all ten shortlisted hotels, every starred restaurant or every activity, and it does not predict service on the February 2027 travel dates. Because some results are walk-only, it is not a complete subway/bus-only matrix. Do not use it to rank all hotels by the number of destinations within one hour; Q5 remains open until the selected hotel and planned departure times are re-routed.

Suggested structure (not a confirmed timetable)

  1. Morning blockA museum or outdoor landmark with published morning hours — e.g. Central Park, The Met (opens 10:00), MoMA (opens 10:30).
  2. Lunch / early afternoonUse a venue near the morning block. The High Line (07:00–20:00 in winter) and Whitney (10:30–18:00 except Tuesday) pair naturally.
  3. Evening blockStarred restaurants with published dinner service. Check the exact day: several close Monday or Tuesday.
  4. BufferAdd transfer time from a real routing result before committing to a reservation.

How to finish this page properly

Once a hotel and itinerary are chosen, rerun each planned trip at its intended departure time with transit constrained. Record the timestamp, mode, minutes and URL; reject walk-only routes when the subway/bus rule is strict. Recheck close to travel and keep only verified pairs at 60 minutes or less. That date-specific matrix—not this “leave now” sample—should drive the final timetable.
