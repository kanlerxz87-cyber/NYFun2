Planning logic

Itinerary & the one-hour travel goal

The request asks to keep activities within one hour of the hotel by subway or bus. Twenty-five door-to-door transit legs were captured from Google Maps on 25 Nov 2026; all are under an hour. This page shows what that does and does not settle.

What is verified

  • Addresses and opening days/hours for the rows where an official page publishes them; all 18 activity rows now carry opening-day text and 27 restaurant rows carry it.
  • The MTA's official subway and bus maps ↗, which identify the lines serving each area.
  • 25 measured legs from five hotels to five Michelin clusters, 3–37 minutes each, with the exact route URL captured for every leg. See Research & integration.
  • Live Google Maps directions links embedded in every restaurant and activity row, using the hotel you select as origin.

What is not verified

The measured minutes are a single snapshot from a routing service, not an official timetable and not a guarantee for February 2027. Four activity legs have a stored route; six activity notes have no captured minutes and are not presented as measured. Re-run the links at your actual departure times before fixing reservations.

Suggested structure (not a confirmed timetable)

  1. Morning blockA museum or outdoor landmark with published morning hours — e.g. Central Park, The Met (opens 10:00), MoMA (opens 10:30).
  2. Lunch / early afternoonUse a venue near the morning block. The High Line (07:00–20:00 in winter) and Whitney (10:30–18:00 except Tuesday) pair naturally.
  3. Evening blockStarred restaurants with published dinner service. Check the exact day: several close Monday or Tuesday.
  4. BufferAdd transfer time from a real routing result before committing to a reservation.

How to finish this page properly

Start from the measured matrix, then route to each shortlisted restaurant and attraction at the intended departure time, record the minutes and the URL, and keep only pairs at 60 minutes or less. Place the one-day Chinatown parade (21 Feb), the Bryant Park close (28 Feb) and Wollman Rink's season end (16 Mar) before the rest of the timetable.
