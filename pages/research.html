How to read these numbers

  • Minutes are a snapshot, not a guarantee. They were read from Google Maps transit directions at one weekday time; subway service changes, weather and the time of day on the trip will alter them.
  • Fare line: the source session recorded $3.00 for subway/local bus and $4.50 for the NYC Ferry leg used for one Williamsburg route. Re-check the official MTA fares page ↗ before travel.
  • Prices are snapshots. A dated aggregator total is not a hotel quote and can change before check-in. Use the linked rate source to reproduce it.
  • Flagged conflicts stay visible. F18–F23 ↗ record the checks that changed or invalidated values from the parallel kits.
