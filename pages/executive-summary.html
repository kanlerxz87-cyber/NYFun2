Bottom line

Plan the shape of the trip now; defer non-refundable commitments that depend on unverified prices. The strongest sourced choices are the Midtown / Flatiron / SoHo / Downtown clusters for a first visit, with hotel selection driven by verified room/bath fit, dated total cost, and cancellation terms. A verified 1-hour transit measurement exists in the 33cfdba4 namespace (25 legs, all ≤37 min across 5 hotels / 5 clusters), but it has not been merged into the canonical hotel shortlist; do not treat canonical hotel ordering as a verified Michelin-within-1-hour ranking.

Key findings

  • Dining: official Michelin counters checked 25 Nov 2026: 5 three-star, 14 two-star, 50 one-star NYC restaurants. The canonical table is a detailed, source-linked subset (21 rows), not the full 69-star census; session 33cfdba4 has a 72-row namespaced roster. Michelin $$$$ band is shown as published — it is not a dollar total for two. Per-couple menu totals remain unverified on official pages for most rows.
  • Hotels: four canonical candidates have dated 7-night totals for 15–22 Feb 2027 (2 adults) captured 2026-11-25 from Booking.com, with exact URLs and tax arithmetic (Hyatt Place NY/Chelsea ~$1,305 all-in; Arlo NoMad ~$1,428; 11 Howard ~$1,774; The Beekman ~$5,220). These are aggregator snapshots, not hotel quotes. A separate 33cfdba4 namespace measured 25 hotel→Michelin-cluster transit legs (all ≤37 min) and priced five 7-night windows; see Hotels for attributed details. Eight canonical candidates remain unpriced.
  • Activities: twelve rows carry official hours. The High Line's published winter window (07:00–20:00, 1 Dec–31 Mar) covers February. MoMA, The Met and the Whitney each publish $30 adult admission, i.e. $60 for two.
  • Social: every restaurant/activity row has at least one social/review link where found; links are labeled third-party unless confirmed on an official venue page/source. This session added confirmed official Instagram/Facebook for Gabriel Kreuther (gknyc.com sameAs) and labeled Instagram accounts for The Modern and Atomix with verification notes. Ownership should still be re-checked on each venue's official site before relying on it.
  • Risk: Michelin awards can change on 14 Dec 2026; restaurant prices for 2027 and hotel rates for 2027 are not published in the sources checked.
