Bottom line

Plan the shape of the trip now; defer non-refundable commitments that depend on unverified prices. Measured transit shows that all five tested Manhattan hotel areas reach all five Michelin clusters within 37 minutes, so the one-hour requirement does not choose a hotel for you. The decision falls to verified bed/bath compliance, dated price and refundable terms: the cheapest qualifying 7-night snapshot in five tested weeks is 15–22 Feb 2027 at $848 pre-tax / ~$998 all-in (Eurostars Wall Street), which also contains the one-day Chinatown parade. No source predicts a future date when rates will be lower.
