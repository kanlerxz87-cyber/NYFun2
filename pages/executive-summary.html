  • Activities: fifteen rows carry official hours, including three date-critical winter 2027 items verified 2026-11-26: the 29th Chinatown Lunar New Year Parade (Sun 21 Feb 2027, 1:00pm, free, organizer's official page), Wollman Rink (28 Oct 2026–16 Mar 2027; Feb 15 = Community Day for NYC public-sector employees & families; 16–21 Feb = Peak $28/adult, 22 Feb–13 Mar = Off-Peak $16/adult, all prices incl. tax) and Bank of America Winter Village at Bryant Park (free rink admission with own skates; closes 28 Feb 2027).
