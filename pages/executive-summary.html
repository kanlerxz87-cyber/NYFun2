Bottom line

Book a free-cancellation hotel now; defer everything non-refundable. The 15–22 Feb week is the cheapest of the five February–March weeks tested (W1 $848 pre-tax / $998 all-in at Eurostars vs $1,087–$1,260 for the March windows), and the 30–39% "Black Friday" rates seen on 25 Nov 2026 expire.
