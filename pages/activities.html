Ferry and queue warning

The National Park Service states that the Statue of Liberty is only accessible by ferry and that security queues can exceed an hour at peak times, including winter holidays (NPS safety page ↗). Presidents' Day falls on 15 Feb 2027, so allow extra time if visiting that day.
