Hotel shortlist

Ten candidates from the Michelin Guide's NYC hotel selection and the citizenM/Marriott brand site. Room facts, bed size and private-bath details are shown only where an official page confirms them.

Price finding — updated 25 Nov 2026: Michelin's own hotel widget states "You cannot book more than 31 days" ahead, and no hotel's own booking engine published a dated February 2027 rate. However, dated rates are obtainable from third-party booking engines: four hotels now carry a live 7-night total for 15–22 Feb 2027 with the exact rate URL and capture date on each row. They are aggregator snapshots, not hotel quotes, and at least one depended on a promotion expiring 31 Dec 2026 (flag F10). Eight of twelve candidates remain unpriced, and the cheapest-booking-window column is still blank because no other week was priced.
