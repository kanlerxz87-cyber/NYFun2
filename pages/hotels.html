Why the ranking is not just "count of restaurants within an hour"

25 legs were measured on 2026-11-25 between five hotels and the five MICHELIN cluster anchors. The longest was 37 minutes (Williamsburg). Because every hotel reached every sampled cluster, the count is identical (5/5) for all measured hotels, and the metric carries no information for choosing between them. The site therefore reports the measured reach as a pass/fail gate and ranks on verified price and room quality. Unmeasured hotels show a blank rather than an implied 5/5.

Best Time to Book Strategy & Reservation Lead Times

1. Hotel Reservation Lead Times

  • Direct Hotel Booking Engines (Hilton, Hyatt, Marriott, CitizenM): Typically release their best direct member and flexible rates 60 to 120 days in advance (approx. October–December 2026 for a February 2027 stay).
  • Online Travel Agencies (OTAs / Booking.com): Currently publish 11-month forward indicative snapshots. Recommended strategy: Lock a flexible, free-cancellation OTA or member rate early, and re-check direct rates 60 days prior to travel.

2. Michelin Dining Reservation Release Windows

  • Tock (EMP, Masa, Per Se, César, Atomix, Atera): Drops reservations on the 1st of each month for the entire subsequent month at 12:00 PM ET (e.g., February 2027 reservations drop on January 1, 2027).
  • Resy (Le Bernardin, Cote, Jua, Gramercy Tavern, Saga): Drops reservations rolling 30 days in advance at 9:00 AM ET (e.g., Feb 15 reservations drop on January 16, 2027).
  • OpenTable (The Modern, Sushi Noz): Drops reservations rolling 30 days in advance at 12:00 AM or 9:00 AM ET.
