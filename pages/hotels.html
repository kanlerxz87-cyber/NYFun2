Why the ranking is not just "count of restaurants within an hour"

25 legs were measured on 2026-11-25 between five hotels and the five MICHELIN cluster anchors. The longest was 37 minutes (Williamsburg). Because every hotel reached every sampled cluster, the count is identical (5/5) for all measured hotels, and the metric carries no information for choosing between them. The site therefore reports the measured reach as a pass/fail gate and ranks on verified price and room quality. Unmeasured hotels show a blank rather than an implied 5/5.
