Audit findings from this integration

  • Stale headline counts (F22). The previous front page claimed 20 restaurant rows, 12 activities and 10 hotels while its own data held 72, 12 and 12; README said 21. All counts are now rendered from JSON at load, and the static HTML matches.
  • Kit rosters were not the official census (F23). 14 kit venues sit outside the 69-star roster, and one kit carried 3 starred names absent from the live list checked on 2026-11-25. None were added; all are in data/conflicts.json.
  • Duplicate rows in merged kits. The same hotel appeared as "The Frederick Hotel Tribeca" and "The Frederick Hotel Tribeca, a Small Luxury Hotel"; NYPL, the Met and 9/11 each appeared twice. The merge now keys these and reports rather than duplicates.
  • Mis-traced transit risk. A fuzzy name match initially attached Eurostars Wall Street's transit legs to The Wall Street Hotel. Matching is now key-based, and unmeasured hotels are blank.
  • Contested prices stay blank (F24). Atera and Per Se are not assigned a price; Atomix carries its verified November Tock price ($848/$628) with December ($990/$704+) flagged; Le Bernardin keeps $218 with the $225 alternative shown.
  • Verification is dated, not implied (F21). 25 checks were run on 2026-11-26 (22 verified, 3 carried captures); everything else is labelled a carried capture.

Parallel sessions and the merge that resolved the collision

Several sessions worked this repository at once. This build started as task T18, then found that arena/547ee3a3-nyfun2 (T18) and arena/45972379-nyfun2 (T21–T23) had already merged PRs #18–#22 to main, making the first PR (#23) conflicting.

Resolution: main was merged into the branch and its datasets were adopted as the new canonical base (research/canonical/*.json), so their verified work — Sushi Sho's Tock price, Per Se's revised deposit pages, the Wollman / Bryant Park / Lunar New Year rows, the official activity socials, and flags F18–F20 — flows into this build. Their pages were regenerated from that merged data rather than overwritten by hand, and the task was renumbered T24 to avoid the T18 collision. Nothing another session verified was discarded; the one file that could not be auto-merged (COORDINATION.md) keeps both sessions' entries.
