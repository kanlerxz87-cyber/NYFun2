What that means for the budget

Two adults taking four rides each per day for seven days would spend 2 × 4 × 7 × $3 = $168. That is an assumption about your behaviour, not a published price, so it is not in the budget total. The fare itself ($3) is verified.

Walking is free and Manhattan below 59th Street is compact: several hotels and starred restaurants in this plan sit within a few blocks of each other.

The one-hour rule, applied to activities

The brief asks that activities sit within about an hour of the hotel. With all four shortlisted hotels in Manhattan below 42nd Street, every verified activity here — The Met (10028), the 9/11 Memorial (10007) and Top of the Rock (10112) — is reachable by a single subway or bus trip from any of them. That statement is about network topology (all three are on the Manhattan grid served by the MTA), not a measured door-to-door time; for measured times use the transit links on the Hotels page and treat the one-hour ceiling as unverified.
