- **No verified 7-night hotel total.** An official Marriott dated search for 15–22 Feb 2027 returned no priced results. Other sites show undated lead rates only. All `7-night total` cells are intentionally blank.
- **No verified ≤1-hour transit counts.** Without a dated door-to-door routing source, travel-time-based Michelin counts cannot be stated. The site shows a clearly-labelled *cluster proxy* instead.
- **No verified 2-adult restaurant totals.** Official menu pages were not retrievable; the Guide only supplies `$`/`$$`/`$$$`/`$$$$` bands. Cost-for-two cells are blank.
- **No 2027 Broadway schedule or 2027 seasonal hours.** Those pages publish current information only.
- **Guide-selected ≠ starred.** Café Carmellini and Cynthia are included in the Guide but their own Guide pages show no star; this distinction is explicit in the table.

## Best-supported planning pattern (not a price claim)

Midtown/Columbus Circle has the largest concentration of Guide entries in this dataset (6), followed by Flatiron/NoMad/Gramercy (5) and Tribeca/SoHo (3). A Midtown or NoMad base therefore minimises the number of neighbourhood changes for dining; this is an address-based observation, **not** a verified travel-time measurement.
