Notes that change the plan

  • A-01 … A-06: items where the venue's own page does not state the value (Met public hours, AMNH ticket price, Statue of Liberty ferry price, 9/11 museum price, NYPL daily hours, Katz's opening hours). These are shown as blank with an 'unverified' chip rather than copied from secondary sources.
  • The High Line's winter hours (07:00–20:00, 1 Dec–31 Mar) shorten February sightseeing days; plan outdoor walks before 20:00.
  • The 9/11 Museum is closed most Tuesdays — if the trip lands on a Tuesday, swap in MoMA (open daily) or the Met.
  • Social-media handles for ACTIVITIES remain unverified. Rather than guess a handle (see the @crownshy trap in flag S-01), the social columns are blank pending review.
