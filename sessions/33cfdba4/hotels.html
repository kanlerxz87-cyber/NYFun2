Hotel flags

  • H-01: Booking.com '1 week, 2 adults' totals generally EXCLUDE taxes (14.75% + $4/night city tax) — real totals are ~16–18% higher.
  • H-02: Some displayed rates are 'Partner Offer' (non-refundable, third-party) or include mandatory resort fees ($38/night at Evelyn and Frederick).
  • H-03: No hotel page explicitly states 'private bathroom' as a filterable attribute in the captured text except through room descriptions; Booking.com does offer a 'Private bathroom' filter (554 properties in NYC at capture). Each shortlisted room lists 'Bath'/'Attached bathroom'/'Private bathroom' — cited per row.
  • H-04: Displayed per-night figures sometimes exclude mandatory fees and therefore do not multiply to the stay total (Benjamin; Evelyn). Totals are the authoritative displayed number.
