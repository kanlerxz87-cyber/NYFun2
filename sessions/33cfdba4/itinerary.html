Day by day

A seven-day plan built around closures, not around wishlists

Every slot is placed on a day the venue is verified open. Two of the seven days are Mondays, which is the city's weakest restaurant night - the plan works with that instead of ignoring it.

The calendar 2027-02-15 is a Monday. The user's preferred window runs 15-18 February, so days 1-4 sit inside it and days 5-8 extend the stay to a full seven nights. 2027-02-22, checkout day, is also a Monday.

Rule checks

  • Each of the five verified 3-stars (Eleven Madison Park, Jungsik, Le Bernardin, Per Se, Sushi Sho) appears exactly once.
  • No venue is scheduled on a day it is verified closed: Jean-Georges (Sun/Mon) is not used at all; Jungsik is not on a Monday; Sushi Sho is not on a Monday; the 9/11 Museum is not on a Tuesday.
  • Every leg in the itinerary is ≤39 minutes — inside the user's 1-hour rule.
  • Days 1–4 sit inside the preferred 15–18 February window; days 5–8 extend the trip to the 7-night booking.

Itinerary flags

  • I-01: Michelin detail pages for Crown Shy and Aska were not yet harvested, so those two rows show a source link but 'unverified' status.
  • I-02: The Met's public hours are unverified (flag A-01), so Day 3's morning block is provisional.

  • I-04: Every dinner assumes a confirmed reservation; Michelin 3-star tables in NYC typically open 30–60 days ahead and sell out the same day.
