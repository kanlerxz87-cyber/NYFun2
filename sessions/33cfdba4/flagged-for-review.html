Flagged for review

Everything this project could not confirm

Nothing here has been silently fixed. Each item says what was found, what the site does about it, and what a reviewer should do next.

F-01 · High severity

Live filter says 5/14/50, the 2025 ceremony article says 5/15/52

Michelin data conflict

guide.michelin.com's live 'all starred' filter reported 69 starred restaurants (5 three-star, 14 two-star, 50 one-star) and 76 Bib Gourmand on 2026-11-25. The official ceremony article for the 2025 selection names 5/15/52. The delta is two rows in the two-star tier and two in the one-star tier.

On the site: All tables follow the LIVE filter counts (5/14/50), because that is the current public selection; the article is cited separately and the discrepancy is stated on the Michelin page.

Next action: Reconcile by name before publishing: diff the two lists row by row and record which restaurant disappeared and when.

F-02 · High severity

The 2026 New York stars do not exist yet

Selection recency

The next MICHELIN Guide New York ceremony is expected around 14 December 2026, after this research date. Every star on the site is therefore the 2025 selection.

On the site: Stated in the page lede and in the executive summary.

Next action: Re-run the starred/bib harvest after the ceremony and regenerate the site from data/restaurants.json.

F-03 · High severity

Booking.com week totals exclude 14.75% tax plus $4 per night

Price comparability

The $848, $943, $1,535, $1,653 and $1,747 totals are displayed before tax. The Eurostars page explicitly showed US$149.66 of tax excluded on top of $848.

On the site: Every hotel row carries a tax_note; totals are labelled pre-tax; the executive summary ranks on the displayed total and says so.

Next action: Add a taxed total column (site suggestion S-03) before the price ranking is treated as final.

F-04 · Medium severity

The Benjamin's per-night figure does not match its stay total

Unreconciled arithmetic

The search result displayed '$174 per night', but the 7-night total was $1,535, which is about $219 per night. Tax treatment on that row was not captured.

On the site: Row marked unverified; the total is treated as authoritative and the per-night value is not shown.

Next action: Open the property page for 2027-02-15 to 02-22 and capture the room table directly.

F-05 · Medium severity

The cheapest room in a search result is often a twin or a full bed

Room-type trap

Eurostars' cheapest 2027-02-22 to 03-01 room was a twin at $836 (fails the queen rule). The Frederick's cheapest room was a full bed at $1,607 (also fails).

On the site: Only queen-or-larger rows are ranked; the failures are documented in hotels.json under 'excluded'.

Next action: Always open the room table, not the search card.

F-06 · High severity

@crownshy on Instagram is not Crown Shy

Social-media trap

instagram.com/crownshy resolves to an account called 'Cutoff' with 4 followers. Likewise instagram.com/jungsik is a personal account ('Jungsik Jin', 24 followers), not the restaurant.

On the site: No handle is shown for either; the rows are blank rather than linking a stranger's account.

Next action: Confirm handles by following the link from the restaurant's own website footer, never by guessing the name.

F-07 · Medium severity

The Met, NYPL, Katz's and AMNH do not publish hours where expected

Missing official hours

metmuseum.org's plan-your-visit page prints only garage hours; the public gallery schedule is rendered by a widget. nypl.org says 'see building hours above' but the widget did not render. katzsdelicatessen.com's menu page carries no opening hours. AMNH publishes hours but not ticket prices on that page.

On the site: Values left blank with a Flagged chip; no hours were copied from a secondary source.

Next action: Capture these from the venue's own hours endpoint or by phone, then fill the blanks.

F-08 · Medium severity

Two Michelin detail pages are still unharvested

Research artefact

Crown Shy and Aska appear in the 1-star / 2-star rosters with correct Michelin URLs but without captured hours, address, phone or booking partner.

On the site: Their rows link to Michelin but show 'unverified'.

Next action: Fetch guide.michelin.com/en/.../restaurant/crown-shy and /aska and append the values.

F-09 · Low severity

Eleven Madison Park's ZIP code differs between sources

Address discrepancy

data/restaurants.json carried 10019; the Michelin detail page shows 10010. The building is at 11 Madison Avenue.

On the site: Not yet corrected in the JSON — the Michelin page value (10010) is the one cited on the site.

Next action: Fix the ZIP in the JSON so the audit trail is clean.

F-10 · Low severity

Only two restaurants have coordinates

Geocoding

Le Bernardin and Per Se were geocoded via Nominatim; the rest of the roster has no lat/lon.

On the site: No map is drawn; the site uses distance-free transit times instead.

Next action: Geocode the roster if the star-map suggestion (S-07) is adopted.

F-11 · Medium severity

All transit times are one weekday-evening sample

Transit variability

Every leg was captured 2026-11-25 between 17:14 and 17:31 ET with 'leave now'. February weather, weekend schedules and overnight headways will change some of them.

On the site: Stated on the Hotels page and in transit_anchors.json's method note.

Next action: Re-run the legs for the actual trip dates before booking.

F-12 · Medium severity

The cheapest rates are a Black Friday promotion, not proof of when to book

Promo vs forecast

Several rates were labelled 'Black Friday Deal' with a validity window of 2026-11-19 to 2027-12-31. That is a dated promotion, not evidence that November is the cheapest time to book.

On the site: pricing_windows.json sets time_to_book = 'unverified' and explains why.

Next action: If a genuine booking-timing forecast is wanted, it needs historical rate tracking, which was out of scope.

F-13 · Low severity

Bib Gourmand and 'Selected' restaurants are counted but not fully tabled

Scope note

76 Bib Gourmand and 224 Selected restaurants were counted from the live site, but only the 69 starred restaurants have individual rows.

On the site: The counts are shown; the table explains the tier difference.

Next action: Expand the Bib roster if the user wants cheaper dining options included in the ranking.

F-14 · Low severity

The itinerary assumes an afternoon arrival

Itinerary assumption

Day 1 places a 17:30 dinner within hours of check-in, which fails if the flight lands late.

On the site: Flagged on the Itinerary page (I-04) and in open question Q-FLIGHT.

Next action: Replace day 1 with a low-commitment option once flights are known.
