Project Goal

Collect, organize, and analyze NYC trip options using verified sources. Every table row should include an official source link; where a value cannot be confirmed from an official source, it is left blank/marked unverified and flagged for review. No values are inferred or fabricated.

Data integrity notice: This dashboard distinguishes Verified, Partially verified, and Unverified information. Live prices, hours, and availability change frequently; always confirm on the linked official source before booking.

What’s Included

Michelin Restaurants

Starred NYC restaurants from the official 2025 MICHELIN Guide list, with star status, cuisine, Michelin page source, official website/social placeholders marked where unverified, and cost notes.

Hotels & Pricing

Hotel shortlist criteria, Michelin-cluster proximity analysis framework, and pricing table. Live 2027 rates are flagged unverified unless confirmed on an official booking page at capture time.

Activities & Social

Curated activities/restaurants with official links, social media review links, hours/open-days fields, and verification status. No invented hours or URLs.

Decision Questions

Clear questions, knowns, unknowns, and missing-information discovery steps needed before final decisions.

Suggestions

Non-destructive improvement suggestions for decision-making, data quality, and site usability. Review before implementation.

Verification & Flags

Line-by-line verification log, irregularities, source audit, and flagged items requiring manual review.
