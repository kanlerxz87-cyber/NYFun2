How “est. all-in” is calculated. Booking.com quotes a pre-tax total that already includes the property’s own resort/facility fee, and lists the taxes separately. This site adds those taxes back: all-in = pre-tax total × (1 + tax %) plus the fixed per-night city, destination and tourism charges. Worked example (H1): $1,113 × 1.1475 + (7 × $4) = $1,305. That is arithmetic on published figures — not a hotel quote. Expect small rounding differences.

MICHELIN proximity — what is actually verified

and the one number this site refuses to invent

The “1 hour or less by subway or bus” rule is not measured here F-01

The brief asks for hotels to be ranked by the number of MICHELIN restaurants reachable within one hour. No transit-routing service or MTA trip planner was reachable from this environment, so no travel time is stated anywhere on this site. What is verified is the postal code on both records — so the column below counts starred venues that share the hotel’s ZIP code, and links a pre-built Google Maps transit query for each pair so you can read the real duration in two clicks.

Same-ZIP is a deliberately conservative proxy: it can only ever under-count. Two venues in adjacent ZIP codes can be a five-minute walk apart. That is why the count is presented as a floor, never as a ranking metric, until a routing source is wired in (suggestion S1).

When to book

what the pricing evidence supports

What this supports: a live promotional discount was active on 25 November 2026 that explicitly covers February 2027 stays (Arlo NoMad: 39% off). The same room on a non-promotional day would cost materially more. What this does not support: any claim that 15–22 February is the cheapest week inside your 15 Feb – 23 Mar window. That comparison was never run (Q-05).

Practical reading

  • Book a free-cancellation rate now while the promotional window is open: it costs nothing to hold and gives you a price floor.
  • The flexibility premium is real and measurable: 11 Howard is +$426 to make refundable ($1,522 → $1,948) and The Beekman +$454 ($4,488 → $4,942) for the same seven nights.
  • Do not treat any figure here as locked until it is re-checked on the hotel’s own engine.

What was ruled out, and why

A price-sorted search for the same dates returned seven-night totals as low as $680 (Hotel 309, Chelsea, “Economy room with shared bathroom”) and $794 (Pod 51, Midtown East, bunk pod with shared bathroom). Both fail the private bathroom requirement, so they were excluded rather than presented as options. Source: Booking.com New York search, 15–22 Feb 2027.

That is the whole point of the filter: the cheap end of the New York market is cheap because it gives up the bathroom or the bed size.
