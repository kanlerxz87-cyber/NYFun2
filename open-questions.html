Budget ceiling for the week High impact

Why it matters. It decides whether the plan can include two or three 3-star dinners. The five verified 3-stars alone would consume most of a mid-range food budget.

Already known. Michelin detail pages often omit tasting-menu prices; only the tier and the cuisine are published.

Still missing. A dinner budget for the couple, and whether tasting menus or a la carte is preferred.

How to find it. Ask each restaurant for its current menu price when the reservation window opens (Resy/Tock/OpenTable show deposits and cancellation terms).

Already known. The roster distinguishes 5 x 3-star, 14 x 2-star, 50 x 1-star and 76 Bib Gourmand.

Why it matters. The brief allows 1-2 weeks. The hotel price research was done for 7 nights; a 14-night stay changes the cheapest window.

Already known. 7 nights 2027-02-15 to 02-22 is the cheapest 7-night window tested; the 02-22 to 03-01 window's cheapest room was a twin (fails the queen rule).

How to find it. Run a Booking.com search for each candidate start date; the Booking Strategy page already tabulates the five windows tested.

How to discover what is missing

  • Call or email the hotel directly for check-in time, bed dimensions, and bathroom fixtures — Booking.com does not publish check-in time in the captured text.
  • Watch Resy/Tock/OpenTable for a specific 3-star: tables usually release 30-60 days before, and the cancellation policy is shown at checkout.
  • Re-run every Google Maps leg the week before travel: 2026-11-25 service was used, and weekend maintenance changes routes.
  • Re-check Michelin after the December 2026 ceremony — one demotion can change the whole hotel ranking.
  • Use the official venue page for hours, not aggregator sites; the Met, Katz's and NYPL all failed to publish hours in a form that could be verified.
