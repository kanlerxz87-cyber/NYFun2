Q-01 to Q-04 are blockers

The four blockers, in plain language

How long are we going? Q-01

Four nights, seven, fourteen, twenty-eight? Every price on this site is seven nights. The brief allows anything from one to four weeks and prefers 15–18 February.

Fix: pick a number. Nothing else can be finalised first.

What is the ceiling? Q-02

A $1,305 stay and a $5,220 stay are both “the best hotel” depending on the budget. The four priced hotels span a 4× range for identical dates.

Fix: state a total, and how much of it is accommodation.

Which criterion wins? Q-03

Price, bed size, or walkability to MICHELIN restaurants. Only one hotel currently has verified same-ZIP proximity to a starred kitchen, and it is not the cheapest.

Fix: rank the three criteria.

What does “private bathroom” mean? Q-04

En-suite, or private-but-separate? It is the difference between a $680 week with a shared bathroom and a $1,305 week with an attached one.

Fix: confirm en-suite.

Missing information, and the fastest route to it

  • Restaurant prices (F-06). Read each restaurant’s own menu page in a browser with JavaScript enabled; record the tasting-menu price per person and its date.
  • Measured transit times (F-01). Either integrate a routing API, or click the Google Maps transit links on the Hotels page and write the duration down.
  • The other 48 one-star and 11 two-star restaurants (F-02). Walk the official filtered list pages, 48 rows at a time, and store name, distinction, cuisine, price band and address.
  • Full weekly hours for The Met and Le Bernardin (F-03, F-07). Re-read the official pages; both publish all seven days.
  • 9/11 Museum ticket price (F-08). One page: the official ticket page.
  • The other candidate weeks (F-09). Repeat four rate checks for each of 22 Feb, 1 Mar, 8 Mar and 15 Mar.
  • Social handles (F-05). Only from each venue’s own site or an official booking-partner page — never from a search result.
  • Weather normals (Q-10). Mid-February conditions decide which of the outdoor candidates stay in the plan.
  • Flights (F-12). Needed before day one can be scheduled honestly.

Questions the site deliberately answers itself

Some things you might expect to be open are already settled by evidence, so they are not on the register:

  • Does a cheap 7-night New York hotel meet the brief? No. The sub-$1,000 options found for your dates had shared bathrooms or bunk beds.
  • Is there a starred restaurant open on a Monday? Yes — Atera ★★ from 7:00 PM, Eleven Madison Park ★★★ from 5:30 PM, Le Bernardin ★★★ a lunch and dinner service, and Per Se ★★★ 4:30–9:00 PM.
  • Is a fully vegan fine-dining option available? Yes — Eleven Madison Park publishes a vegan path on its tasting menu, and MICHELIN lists it as contemporary with vegan options.
  • Can a starred dinner be booked without a credit card hold? Not at Le Bernardin: a card is required and the cancellation window is 48 hours at $150 per person.
