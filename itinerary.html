Seven nights, built around verified opening hours

Each dinner is placed only on a day the kitchen is verifiably open, using the hours published on the MICHELIN records. Where an hour is unverified, the day is kept flexible and the gap is marked. 15 Feb 2027 is a Monday and 22 Feb 2027 is a Monday — seven nights exactly.

Preferred window: 15–18 Feb (4 nights) Recommended: 15–22 Feb (7 nights) Extendable to 14 nights (15 Feb–1 Mar)

Two lengths, one plan

The preferred 4-night version

15–18 Feb 2027 keeps Days 1–4 below. It covers three starred dinners — Atera ★★, Eleven Madison Park ★★★ and Le Bernardin ★★★ — plus The Met, Central Park and the 9/11 Memorial & Museum.

Hotel cost for 4 nights is exactly half of a 7-night figure only if the nightly rate holds; weekly and promotional rates are not linear, so re-price before booking (Q-01).

The recommended 7-night version

Adds Per Se ★★★, Top of the Rock, a Brooklyn Bridge morning and a deliberately empty Sunday. Seven nights is the length every price on this site was captured at, so it is the only length whose numbers are internally consistent.

Going to 14 nights (15 Feb – 1 Mar) roughly doubles the hotel line and adds a second week of dinners — not costed here (F-13).

A defensible envelope for the “known” part

Hotel + The Met + Top of the Rock = $1,449 all-in for two, on the cheapest compliant hotel. Everything else is an open variable. If you need a hard ceiling for the whole trip, set it and the hotel choice follows automatically — that is Q-02.

Bookings, in the order they should be made

  1. Decide the length and the ceiling — 4, 7, 14 or more nights; and a total budget (Q-01, Q-02).
  2. Hold a free-cancellation hotel room — Hyatt Place NY/Chelsea is the cheapest compliant rate and cancels free until 13 Feb 2027. Holding costs nothing and sets a price floor.
  3. Open a Resy / Tock account now — Le Bernardin and Eleven Madison Park book on Resy; Per Se and Atera book on Tock (links on the MICHELIN page). Starred tables in New York release weeks ahead and a February weekend can go quickly.
  4. Book Atera ★★ for Monday 15 Feb — its Monday service starts at 7:00 PM, which only works if your flight lands in the morning.
  5. Confirm The Met’s closed day, then fix the Tuesday plan.
  6. Re-price the hotel on its own site and cancel the hold if the direct rate is better.
