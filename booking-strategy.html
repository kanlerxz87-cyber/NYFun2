Price flags

  • P-01: Displayed '1 week, 2 adults' totals exclude taxes (14.75% + $4/night city tax) — true cost is higher. Booking room tables state this explicitly.
  • P-02: Some cheapest rooms in a window are twin/full beds (fail the queen-bed requirement) and are labelled as such.
  • P-03: '79% of places to stay are unavailable' banner on Booking.com for some windows indicates limited inventory in the 4–5 star band; treat all totals as volatile.
