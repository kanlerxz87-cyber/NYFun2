Recommended trip, in one paragraph

based only on verified facts

Base: Hyatt Place New York/Chelsea at an estimated $1,305 all-in for seven nights — it is the cheapest of the four priced hotels, it has a King bed and an attached bathroom, and the rate includes breakfast and free cancellation until 13 February 2027. Upgrade if MICHELIN walkability is worth ~$120: Arlo NoMad at about $1,428 all-in with a Queen Room, described by Booking as roughly 1,056 ft from the Empire State Building and the 6 train. Dinners: one per night across Atera ★★ (Tribeca), Eleven Madison Park ★★★ (Flatiron), Le Bernardin ★★★ (Midtown) and Per Se ★★★ (Columbus Circle) — the four kitchens whose opening hours for your exact weekdays were verified. Days: The Met ($30 each), the free 9/11 Memorial plus the Museum (open Wednesday), and Top of the Rock (from $42 each). Not yet costed: flights, restaurant bills and theatre tickets.
