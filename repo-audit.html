Method notes

  • Counts come from paginated listings: the all-starred page truncates at 48 rows, so page 2 was counted separately.
  • Booking.com room tables were read at room level; a hotel's cheapest advertised room is frequently a twin or full bed and therefore fails the queen requirement.
  • Transit times are the fastest itinerary Google returned at capture, not the default selected option.
  • Where a venue's own page omitted a value, the cell is blank. Secondary sources were deliberately not used to fill it.
